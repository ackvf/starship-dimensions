The **Aurora Needle** is a deep-range relay cruiser built to map unstable wormlight corridors.

## Flight profile

- Optimized for long drift-window operations
- Carries modular sensor pods instead of heavy weapons
- Uses a tri-spine radiator lattice visible during full burn

## Trivia

- The Aurora Needle was the first ship to successfully navigate the Helios Drift wormlight corridors.
- Its unique tri-spine radiator lattice design has become iconic among deep-range relay cruisers.
